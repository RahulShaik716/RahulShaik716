# About Me

I’m a full-stack software engineer with 7+ years of experience building web and mobile products used by real customers.

I started my career building enterprise applications at Shell, where I worked across React, React Native, Node.js, Java, .NET, APIs, and cloud services. Today, I work on customer-facing products spanning payments, financing, scheduling, and internal business workflows, taking features from an idea to production.

Lately, I’ve been spending more of my time exploring AI-native products. I enjoy building with LLMs, experimenting with agents and tool calling, and figuring out how to turn AI capabilities into products that are genuinely useful rather than just technically interesting.

I care about good engineering, simple user experiences, and understanding the problem before writing the code. I’m particularly interested in the intersection of full-stack engineering and AI, and I use this space to build, experiment, and share what I learn.
